Before Your Visit

Quick answers about appointments, privacy, reports, treatment planning and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions
Is an appointment recommended?⌄
Yes. Appointment booking is recommended so sufficient consultation time can be reserved. Walk-in availability may vary.
Are consultations confidential?⌄
Yes. Personal and clinical information is handled with appropriate privacy. Sensitive consultations are conducted discreetly.
What should I bring?⌄
Bring relevant previous prescriptions, laboratory reports, scans, medication lists and any records related to your current concern.
How long does a consultation take?⌄
Duration depends on the type and complexity of the consultation. Detailed assessments or procedures may require additional time.
Will treatment start on the first visit?⌄
Not always. The first priority is assessment and suitability. Treatment may begin on the same day when appropriate, or a separate session may be advised.
How is a treatment plan decided?⌄
Recommendations are individualized according to history, examination or assessment findings, goals, suitability, available evidence and follow-up needs.
Are tests always required?⌄
No. Investigations are recommended only when they are clinically useful for evaluation, safety or treatment planning.
Can I attend an online consultation?⌄
Selected consultations and follow-ups may be suitable for online care. Procedures and concerns requiring physical examination need an in-clinic visit.
Can I ask about fees before booking?⌄
Yes. Published fees can be reviewed on the relevant service pages. Final treatment plans and package components may depend on clinical assessment.
Are results guaranteed?⌄
No. Individual responses vary. The clinic does not guarantee a particular clinical, aesthetic or wellness outcome.
